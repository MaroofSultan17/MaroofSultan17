### About me

Full stack Software Engineer with 2.5+ years of experience building SaaS, streaming and eCommerce products with Laravel, Symfony, Node.js and React. I enjoy the parts of a product that have to be reliable: payment flows, background queues, video pipelines and fast database queries.

- 🎬 **Now:** Software Engineer at **[Buggcy](https://buggcy.com)**, building **[FilmLauncher](https://filmlauncher.com)**, a MERN streaming and crowdfunding platform for independent films
- 🛒 **Before:** Software Engineer at **[AlphaSoft360](https://www.alphasoft360.com/team/maroof-sultan)**, worked on a wholesale store with 7K+ users and 40K+ products
- 🧩 **Part time:** Senior Laravel Developer at **[HiddenLogics](https://hiddenlogics.com)** (2024 to 2026)
- 🎓 BS Computer Science, **COMSATS University Islamabad**, CGPA 3.80
- 🏅 Microsoft Certified: **Azure AI Fundamentals**
- 🔐 Currently learning more about **application security**
